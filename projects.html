Letter Roll

2026

Tap the dice, get a letter, then name five things from a surprise category. The die is a real 26-sided solid — a rhombicuboctahedron, eighteen squares and eight triangles, one letter per face — built, tumbled, lit and shaded from scratch on a canvas, with motion blur and a shadow that tracks its hop. Roll as many times as you like; the letter only locks when you start typing, and that is when one of the day's two games is used up. Works offline.

Word Snakes

2026

A winding word puzzle in the style of the New York Times' Strands. Six puzzles, each on a six by eight board where every single letter belongs to one answer and nothing is left over — including a spangram that names the theme and stretches right across the grid. The words come from the books the boys read: Cakes in Space, Swallows and Amazons, Katherine Rundell, the Famous Five and the Secret Seven, plus one made of our own family. Drag or tap to trace a word; spot three ordinary words and you earn a hint. Works offline.
